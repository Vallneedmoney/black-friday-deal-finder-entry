How we judge “is it really a deal?”

  • REAL DEAL — a genuine steal: 40% or more off, or the lowest price we can point at. Eight of the twelve deals here earn this.
  • STRONG PRICE — a solid saving of 20–39% on a real list price. Worth buying if you wanted it anyway.
  • MODEST CUT — a real cut under 20%. Honest, but no reason to rush. Only one deal here lands here — the Switch 2 bundle, whose first-ever $50 cut mostly just makes the game free.

Each card names its source and the hour its price was read. “Was” prices are the store’s own list or comparable value, never a number we made up. Prices move all day on Black Friday — always re-check at checkout before you pay.
